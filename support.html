Hand & Foot Support

Need help starting a game, inviting a friend, or using Hand & Foot? Here's where to begin.

Contact support

Email handandfootfeedback@gmail.com.

For a game issue, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app build number (if known), what you were trying to do, and any error message. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

How do I play?

On the main menu, choose Play Against Computer to play with a computer teammate, Play Together Here to pass one iPhone between four people, or Play With Friends Online to invite people on their own iPhones.

How do I invite a friend online?

Choose Play With Friends Online, sign in to Apple Game Center if prompted, and tap Invite a Friend. Game Center handles the invitation. To play on the same team or invite three friends, expand More Ways to Play.

My friend's invitation or turn isn't showing

Check that you are both signed in to Game Center on your own iPhones, have the current app version installed, and have an internet connection. Reopen Play With Friends Online, look under Your Games, and tap Refresh. If the invitation still doesn't appear, contact support with the details above.

How do I change names or local game settings?

Tap Game Settings on the main menu. You can adjust names, local seating options, and private handoffs there. Private handoffs hide cards when passing the phone to the next player.

Can I resume a game?

Local games save automatically and can be resumed from Continue Saved Game on the main menu. Online games are stored through Apple Game Center and appear in Your Games.
