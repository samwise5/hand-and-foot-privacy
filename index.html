Sam's Hand & Foot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026   Developer: Hand & Foot (independent app developer)   Contact: handandfootfeedback@gmail.com

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the Sam's Hand & Foot iOS app handles information in version 1.0.

Information stored on your device

Sam's Hand & Foot lets you enter player names, play games, save your progress, and keep a local record of wins, losses, and ties. The app stores these details, together with game settings and gameplay state, locally on your device so you can resume games and view your results.

The developer does not receive this locally stored information through the app. You do not need to create an account to use the available game modes.

Information we collect

In version 1.0, Sam's Hand & Foot does not collect or transmit personal information or gameplay data to the developer's servers. The app does not include third-party advertising, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Online Game Center multiplayer is not available in version 1.0. If it is enabled in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated to explain that functionality.

Apple services and device backups

Apple may process information associated with App Store downloads, purchases, device diagnostics, and device backups under Apple's own policies and your device settings. If iCloud Backup is enabled, locally stored app data may be included in backups managed by Apple. For information about Apple's practices, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support emails

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we will receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use those details only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, and manage the correspondence. We do not sell support messages or use them for advertising. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing handandfootfeedback@gmail.com, subject to any legal requirements to retain it.

Data retention and deletion

Local saved games, player names, and score history remain on your device until they are replaced or the app's data is removed. You can delete locally stored Sam's Hand & Foot information by using Delete App in iOS (rather than Offload App). Depending on your settings, copies in device or iCloud backups may remain until those backups expire or are deleted through Apple's controls.

Because the developer does not receive in-app saved games, we cannot remotely view or delete them.

Children's privacy

Sam's Hand & Foot is a family card game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If a child sends information in a support email and you would like it deleted, please contact us at handandfootfeedback@gmail.com.

Security

The app uses iOS-provided local storage and file protection for saved game data. No method of storing data is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Privacy policy website hosting

This publicly available privacy policy page may be served through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic connection information, such as IP addresses, when visitors access the page for security and operational purposes. This website hosting activity is separate from data collected by the Hand & Foot app. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at this location with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: handandfootfeedback@gmail.com
