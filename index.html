Sam's Hand & Foot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026   Developer: Hand & Foot (independent app developer)   Contact: handandfootfeedback@gmail.com

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the Sam's Hand & Foot iOS app handles information in version 1.0.

Information stored on your device

When you play against the computer or share one iPhone in Pass & Play, the app stores player names, local saved games, settings, scores, and win/loss/tie records on your device. This lets you resume local games and view your results.

The developer does not receive this local game information through a developer-operated server. You do not need a separate account for local play. Online play uses Apple Game Center.

Information handled for online multiplayer

The app does not use developer-operated multiplayer servers, third-party advertising, tracking SDKs, or third-party analytics. The app does not request access to your location, photos, camera, or microphone.

Apple Game Center: If you choose Play With Friends Online, Apple's Game Center handles player sign-in, invitations, matchmaking, and turn-based match storage. The app uses your Game Center display name and player identifier to associate you with a player seat.

Online match data: The app sends game state to Apple's Game Center service to continue online matches. This can include player names, all players' hands and feet, melds, books, scores, and turn history. Game Center shares match data with match participants. The ordinary game interface hides opponents' private cards, but the underlying shared match data includes the complete game state, including cards not normally displayed to other players. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information as player names or game information.

The developer does not operate a separate server that receives Game Center match data and does not use match information for advertising. For Apple's data practices, see Game Center privacy information and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple services and device backups

Apple may process information associated with App Store downloads, purchases, device diagnostics, and device backups under Apple's own policies and your device settings. If iCloud Backup is enabled, locally stored app data may be included in backups managed by Apple. For information about Apple's practices, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support emails

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we will receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use those details only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, and manage the correspondence. We do not sell support messages or use them for advertising. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing handandfootfeedback@gmail.com, subject to any legal requirements to retain it.

Data retention and deletion

Local saves, player names, and score history remain on your iPhone until replaced or removed. To delete locally stored game data, use Delete App in iOS (not Offload App). Device and iCloud backups may retain earlier copies according to Apple's controls.

Online matches are stored and managed by Apple Game Center. Deleting the app does not necessarily remove those Game Center match records. The developer cannot remotely read or delete Game Center matches or your local saved games from a developer-operated server.

Children's privacy

Sam's Hand & Foot is a family card game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated servers. Apple Game Center accounts and family controls apply to online play. If a child sends information in a support email and you would like it deleted, please contact us at handandfootfeedback@gmail.com.

Security

The app uses iOS-provided local storage and file protection for saved game data. No method of storing data is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Privacy policy website hosting

This publicly available privacy policy page may be served through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic connection information, such as IP addresses, when visitors access the page for security and operational purposes. This website hosting activity is separate from data collected by the Hand & Foot app. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at this location with a new effective date.
